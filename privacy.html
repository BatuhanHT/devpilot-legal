DevPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

DevPilot is an AI assistant for software engineers. This policy explains what data the app handles, who receives it, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, display name and engineering role. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored only as a hash.
  • Content you create: your chat conversations and messages, saved code snippets (code library), and your projects (name, description, language and linked repository address).
  • Usage records: timestamps of your AI requests, kept only to enforce rate limits and removed automatically after one day.
  • On your device only: your app language choice and, if you add one, your GitHub access token (stored in the iOS Keychain and never sent to our servers).

How AI features use your data

When you chat or analyze code, DevPilot sends your message, your recent conversation history and any code or repository file content you attach to NVIDIA NIM, the AI inference service we use, through our own server. This is the only way answers are generated. This happens only after you give consent, which you can withdraw in Settings → Privacy & Data.

Other services

  • Supabase: hosts authentication and our database.
  • NVIDIA NIM: generates AI responses (see above).
  • GitHub: if you link a repository, the app reads its files from GitHub. Private repositories are accessed with your own token, directly from your device.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, do not sell your data and do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, conversations, messages, code library, projects and profile from our servers. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

Children

DevPilot is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
